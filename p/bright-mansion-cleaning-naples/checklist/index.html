Standard & recurring cleaning

The core of every visit to an occupied home.

Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash wiped
  • Sink scrubbed and shined
  • Appliance exteriors and microwave
  • Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash taken out

Bathrooms

  • Toilet cleaned inside and out
  • Shower, tub and glass
  • Sink, faucet and counter
  • Mirrors streak-free
  • Floors washed
  • Towels folded

Bedrooms

  • Beds made, linens changed on request
  • Surfaces and nightstands dusted
  • Mirrors and glass
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped
  • Light tidy-up

Living areas

  • Furniture and shelves dusted
  • Cushions straightened
  • Glass tables and mirrors
  • Rugs and floors vacuumed
  • Lamps and decor wiped

Dining

  • Table and chairs wiped
  • Light fixtures dusted
  • Sideboards and shelves
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Entry & hallways

  • Entry table and decor
  • Door handles and switches
  • Stairs and railings
  • Floors and entry rug

Deep cleaning

Everything above, plus

Ideal for a first visit or a seasonal reset.

  • Baseboards, doors and trim by hand
  • Grout lines and shower fixtures
  • Window sills and tracks
  • Kitchen cabinet fronts and backsplash detail
  • Light switches and outlet covers
  • Vents and ceiling fan blades

Move-in & move-out

For an empty home

Share your walkthrough date and any lease requirements.

  • Inside all cabinets and drawers
  • Inside fridge, oven and microwave
  • Closets, shelves and storage areas
  • Walls spot-cleaned
  • Garage sweep on request
  • Ready for the final walkthrough

Extras

Add what you need

Request any of these when you ask for your quote.

  • Inside the oven
  • Inside the refrigerator
  • Interior windows
  • Laundry, wash and fold
  • Organizing a closet or pantry
  • Patio or balcony sweep
