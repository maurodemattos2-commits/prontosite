Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients share a door code or leave a key. We'll agree on the best way to get in before the first visit.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, we bring the supplies and equipment. If you prefer a specific product, just let us know and we'll use it.

How is the price calculated?

Every home is different, so we quote based on size, condition and how often you'd like us to come. Send a few details and we'll reply with a free quote.

What if something isn't right?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back to make it right. Your satisfaction is the whole point.

Can I keep the same day and team?

For recurring service we keep a regular day and the same team whenever possible, so they learn how you like your home.
